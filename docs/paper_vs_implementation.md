# Paper versus Public-Data Implementation

The machine-readable comparison is `results/publication_tables/paper_vs_public_data_implementation.csv`.

The closest methodological matches are chronological validation, validation-only model selection, the zero-benchmark OOS R-squared formula, monthly prediction sorts, and drift-adjusted turnover. Major differences are the current-survivor universe, short period, raw-return target, 15 predictors rather than 920, missing macro/industry inputs, and equal rather than value weighting.

These differences prevent direct numerical comparison or any claim that this project reproduced or outperformed GKX. The meaningful contribution is an independently implemented, leakage-controlled public-data adaptation with transparent negative economic findings.
