# Limitations

- The fixed 497-security universe uses surviving current constituents, not point-in-time historical membership.
- Survivorship and membership look-ahead bias remain unresolved.
- Delisted securities and delisting returns are absent.
- The target is raw adjusted-price return rather than CRSP excess return.
- Fifteen public price/volume predictors replace the paper’s 920-variable firm/macro/industry design.
- Synthetic local identifiers are not CRSP PERMNOs.
- Equal-weight deciles replace the paper’s principal value-weighted portfolios because reliable lagged market capitalization is unavailable.
- The locked test spans only 34 months, limiting HAC, bootstrap, and factor-regression power.
- Factor analysis is post-hoc and does not establish statistically significant or implementable alpha.
- The transaction-cost grid excludes borrowing, financing, asymmetric spreads, and market impact.
- The public candidate omits the complete research panel, predictions, and weights for redistribution and size reasons.

Results apply only to the fixed public-data universe of 497 surviving securities. They do not represent the historical S&P 500 or broad U.S. equity market and are not evidence of a profitable investment strategy.
